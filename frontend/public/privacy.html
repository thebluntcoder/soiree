Privacy Policy

Draft — last updated 11 September 2026

Soirée is an AI concierge that plans an evening — a date, a dinner with friends, a birthday — using your Swiggy account for restaurants, groceries and offers, and Anthropic's Claude to write the plan. This page explains what we collect, why, and how to get rid of it.

How you sign in

There is no Soirée password. Signing in means authorising Soirée against your Swiggy account through Swiggy's own login page (Swiggy handles your phone number and OTP directly — we never see either). What comes back is an access token that lets Soirée call Swiggy's Food, Instamart and Dineout APIs on your behalf, and a stable identifier for your account that we use to recognise you on future visits.

We do not collect payment details — ordering isn't built yet — and we never see your Swiggy password or OTP.

Where it lives

  • Postgres — your account, events and generated plans. Kept until you delete your account.
  • Redis — your Swiggy access token (encrypted at rest, expires automatically after 5 days) and your Soirée session token (expires after 30 days or on sign-out).

Who else sees it

  • Swiggy — every Food/Instamart/Dineout call Soirée makes is made as you, with your token.
  • Anthropic (Claude) — your event details and chat messages are sent to Claude's API to generate and refine plans, under Anthropic's own privacy terms. Soirée does not send your Swiggy token or account identifier to Claude.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Your rights

See what we hold: GET /users/me from the app, or ask. Delete everything — your account, every event, every plan, your Swiggy token, your session — in one action from the account menu in the app. It's immediate and there's no undo.

Security

The Swiggy token is encrypted at rest; all traffic runs over HTTPS; sign-in and plan-generation endpoints are rate-limited. Like any software, this is not a guarantee against every possible failure — treat it accordingly.

Who this is for

Soirée is not directed at children. Some event types (e.g. alcohol preference) assume an adult user; don't use it if you're under the legal drinking age in your state.

Changes

This is a young, actively-developed product — this page will change as features (ordering, group planning) are added. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date.
